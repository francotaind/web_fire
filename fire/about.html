About AXESS Plumbers and Safety Systems

Your trusted partner for plumbing and fire safety solutions since 2005

Our Story

Founded in 2005 by John Smith, AXESS PLUMBERS AND SAFETY SYSTEMS LTD began as a small plumbing service operating in South London. Recognizing the growing need for integrated plumbing and fire safety solutions, we expanded our services to become a one-stop shop for property owners and managers.

Today, we serve residential and commercial clients across London and the Southeast, with a team of over 30 certified professionals. Our commitment to quality, safety, and customer service has made us a trusted name in the industry.

Our Mission

At AXESS, our mission is to provide reliable, high-quality plumbing and fire safety solutions that protect people and properties. We believe in:

  • Delivering exceptional service with integrity and professionalism
  • Using only the highest quality materials and equipment
  • Ensuring all our work meets or exceeds safety standards
  • Building long-term relationships with our clients
  • Continually improving our skills and services
